Your agents spend. You hold the reins.

AI agents get small TON wallets of their own. Anything beyond their rules waits for one look at your Even G2 glasses.

Set it upWhat it stops

Three steps, then you mostly just glance.

  1. Create a vault

    Open Reins in the Even app. It makes a TON wallet whose key never leaves your phone, and you top it up from any TON wallet.

  2. Connect an agent

    Give Claude Code, Hermes, Codex or your own agent a one-time code. It asks your glasses for a wallet and gets a small starting buffer.

  3. Let it work

    Trusted payees under their limit go through on their own. Anything new, odd or over budget shows up on the lens with a one-word verdict.

Built for the ways agents actually get fooled.

We ran real models against it. They paid addresses planted in documents and followed instructions hidden in invoices. Each of these was caught on TON testnet.

Address poisoning

The address copies the first and last letters of a payee you trust. Reins compares it with your payees and shows both prints.

Injected amounts

The agent wrote "0.4" in its reason and asked for 2. Reins reads the amount in the reason and compares, in code, not with another model.

A stolen agent key

If someone signs with the agent's key directly, the watchdog sees money leave without an approval. Kill revokes the key on chain and sweeps the rest back.

An agent that isn't yours

A new key asking for a wallet under the name of an agent you already run is flagged before it gets anything.

Any agent that speaks MCP.

The agent gets three tools: connect, wallet and pay. It never sees a key that can touch your vault, and every call it makes is signed with its own.

  • Real wallets. Agents use TON's agentic wallet contract. Revoking one is a transaction, not a database flag.
  • Your key stays home. The vault key lives on your phone. The Reins server relays requests and watches the chain; it cannot move your money.
  • Telegram, too. Warnings arrive in Telegram with Reject and Pause buttons. Approving still takes your glasses.
  • Voice. Say "approve", "why" or "give trip-bot one more". Flagged requests can't be approved by voice.
