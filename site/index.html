How it works

Set it up once. Then just glance.

Your vault stays on your phone. Each agent gets a small wallet. The glasses are where you say yes.

STEP 1

Create a vault

Open Reins in the Even app. Your vault's key is made on your phone and never leaves it. Top it up from any TON wallet.

STEP 2

Connect an agent

Give Claude Code, Hermes, Codex or your own agent a one-time code. It asks your glasses for a wallet.

STEP 3

Let it work

Trusted payees under their limit just get paid. Everything else lands on the lens with a one-word verdict.

Injected amounts

An instruction hidden in a document changes what the agent asks for. Reins reads the amount in the agent's own reason and compares.

    Stolen keys
