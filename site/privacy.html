REINS

Privacy

Last updated November 2026. Reins is built and run by Miracle (github.com/miracle352). The hosted server is at this address.

What stays on your phone

Your vault key (the 24 words) is created and stored on your phone, in the Even Realities app's storage for Reins. It is never sent to the Reins server or anywhere else.

What the Reins server stores

For each vault: its public address, your agents' names, public keys and wallet addresses, payment requests and their outcome, payees you trust and the names you gave them, daily budgets, a log of recent events, and, if you link it, your Telegram chat id. Login tokens are stored only as hashes. This is what Reins needs to show you requests and to watch your agents' wallets. Nothing is sold or shared.

Permissions the app asks for

Network: to talk to the Reins server, which relays your agents' requests and reads TON testnet.

Glasses microphone: only while a request or a voice prompt is on your glasses, for a few seconds, so you can say "approve", "reject" or "why". The clip goes to the Reins server, which turns it into text with Groq's Whisper service (on the hosted server) or a Whisper model on the server itself (if you host Reins yourself). Clips are not stored. Groq's own policy: groq.com/privacy-policy.

Other services

The server reads the TON blockchain through toncenter.com. Telegram alerts go through Telegram's Bot API. Data is kept in a Postgres database hosted by Neon, and the server runs on Render.

Deleting your data

Send /unlink to the bot to unlink Telegram. To remove everything about your vault from the server, open an issue on the GitHub repository or message the developer, and it will be deleted. Payments on the blockchain are public and can't be deleted by anyone.
